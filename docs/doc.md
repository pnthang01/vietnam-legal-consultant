# Analysis of LKIF Core Ontologies
## LKIF core ontologies consist of 15 modules, which include:

### Abstract Concepts
- top: General concepts derived from LRI-Core with limited ontological constraints.
- place: Implementing spatial relationships (Donnelly, 2005).
- mereology: Relationships of parts and wholes.
- time: Temporal relationships (Allen’s theory, 1984).
### Basic Concepts
- process: Change, causal changes, and objects involved.
- role: Roles and their properties.
- action: Agents and their performed actions.
- expression: Propositions, propositional attitudes, norms, and qualifications.
### Legal Concepts
- legal-action: Legal actions, agents, public acts, legal persons.
- legal-role: Roles specific to legal professions.
- norm: Normative qualifications, rights, and powers.
### Framework Modules
- modification: Extension of time and legal-action, modifications of legal documents.
- rules: Argumentation and rules according to LKIF standards.
